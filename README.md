# Ranknix Online - Digital Agency Official Website

Yeh **Ranknix Online** ke liye custom code par banai gayi mukammal, modern, aur high-converting agency website hai. Iska design `onlinekrlo.pk` jaisa clean white, bright aur animated banaya gaya hai.

---

## 🌟 Key Updates & Features (Naye Features)

1. **Clean White & Bright Modern Theme:**
   - Background ko bright white & soft light-blue tint par convert kar diya gaya hai.
   - Clean white cards, gentle elevated shadows, aur vibrant electric blue accents (`#2563eb`).
2. **Smooth Scroll & Micro Animations:**
   - **AOS (Animate on Scroll):** Scroll karte waqt sections smooth fade-up aur slide-in hotay hain.
   - **Floating Elements:** Founder photo ke sath animated live floating badges (e.g. *"₨ 65M+ Revenue Scaled"*, *"⭐️ 4.9/5 Rating"*).
   - **Stats Counter:** Live numbers count-up hotay hain.
   - **Brand Ticker:** Infinite marquee loop (Meta, TikTok, Shopify, Google Ads).
3. **Smart Auto Geo-Currency System:**
   - **Pakistan Se Search Karne Par:** Auto-detect ho kar **₨ (PKR)** prices show hongi.
   - **UK Se Search Karne Par:** Auto-detect ho kar **£ (GBP)** prices show hongi.
   - **US / Rest of World:** **$ (USD)** aur **€ (EUR)** show hongi.
   - **Header Currency Switcher:** Navbar ke top par currency dropdown diya gaya hai taake koi bhi user khud bhi currency switch kar sake.
4. **Branding & Images:**
   - **Transparent Logo:** Aapka logo background remove karke transparent PNG format mein header aur footer dono jagah lagaya gaya hai:
     - **Ranknix Online**
     - **DIGITAL AGENCY**
   - **Founder Photo:** Hero section mein aur dedicated *"Leadership & Vision"* section mein founder ki photo professional framed look aur floating badges ke sath shamil ki gayi hai.
5. **Direct WhatsApp Integration:**
   - Number updated: **`+92 308 8163940`** (`wa.me/923088163940`)
   - Official Email: **`contact@ranknix.online`**
   - Floating pulsing WhatsApp button.
   - Har package aur service ka pre-filled custom WhatsApp message.
